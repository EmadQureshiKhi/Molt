Three things about the geometry are deliberate, and each corrects a way the diagram was unreadable. Every label is styled by the stylesheet rather than left to a default. An SVG `text` element with no fill is painted black, so on any surface that is not white the labels were invisible — present in the markup, selectable, and impossible to see. A node is as wide as its longest label rather than a fixed width. Kinds are words like `derived_artifact`, which at a readable size is far wider than the box that used to hold it, so the text ran out through both sides of its own shape.
